# TRIBE Research Project — Public Record

**Status:** Ongoing independent research  
**First public record:** September 2026  
**Researcher:** Tiago Ribeiro

TRIBE is an ongoing independent research project focused on the study and development of advanced local AI agents.

The research investigates how AI agents can become more reliable, efficient, adaptive, and effective in long-running tasks while operating with local computing resources.

Current areas of investigation include, at a high level:

- local AI-agent performance;
- memory and continuity across tasks;
- learning from prior work;
- evaluation and benchmarking;
- efficient use of computing resources;
- reliability and verification of agent outputs;
- long-horizon task execution.

This public record is intentionally limited to the existence, scope, and research objectives of the project.

**Technical architecture, implementation methods, internal mechanisms, experimental datasets, training procedures, source code, unpublished results, and proprietary research details are not disclosed in this public record.**

The purpose of this page is to document that the TRIBE research project is active and under development without publishing its technical implementation.

---

**Research status:** Experimental and ongoing. No claim of superiority over existing AI systems is made without reproducible benchmark evidence.
